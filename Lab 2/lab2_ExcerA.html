About Event

AVAILABLE ACTIVITIES

UMT Career Fair have prepared various activities for students of varying years to participate in, most notably:

  • From study to industry: how to transition into workspace.
  • Alumni Talk: Speech given by successful graduate of university on various tips
  • How to Resume: A workshop instructing how to make your resume pops out.

Notable Speaker:

  • En. Ahmad Jazmi Bin Nukman: Recruiters for Tenaga Nasional Berhad
  • En. Muhammad Afiq Nazhan: UMT graduate currently working in aviation industry
